Manmohan Nanda v. United India Insurance Co. Ltd. (2022), Supreme Court of India.

Holding: Where an insurer evaluates a proposer, seeks medical information, and issues
a policy despite a disclosed underlying condition (there, Type-II diabetes), it cannot
later repudiate a claim by relying on that disclosed condition or its direct
complications. Disclosure made and accepted at proposal cannot be re-characterised as
non-disclosure to defeat the claim.
